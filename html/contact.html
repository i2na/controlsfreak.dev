Contact

Feedback and error reports Korean edition

Found an awkward translation or a tool that isn't working? Let i2na, the maintainer of this Korean edition, know.

Send feedback on GitHub ↗

A GitHub account is required.

What to include

  • Page address — a link to the page with the problem.
  • Translation errors — the original and translated wording, and your suggested correction.
  • Tool errors — inputs, units, expected and actual results, and the steps that led to the problem.

Your post will be public. Please leave out passwords, personal contact details, and private site information.

Feedback goes to the Korean edition's maintainer. The original author operates Controls Freak separately.
